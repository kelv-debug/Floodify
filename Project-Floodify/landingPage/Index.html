Floodify's Aim

Making Flood Hazard Information Easier to See and Understand

Floodify aims to transform complex flood hazard information into an interactive visual experience, helping users explore areas that may be exposed to significant flooding.

The 100-Year Flood Scenario

A Standardized View of Significant Flood Hazard

Floodify uses the 100-year flood scenario as its primary reference for flood hazard visualization. This scenario represents a flood event with a 1% annual probability of being equaled or exceeded, providing a consistent way to visualize areas that may be vulnerable to major flooding.

Why Meycauayan floods

Meycauayan sits downstream of the Angat, Ipo, and Bustos dams. When heavy rain forces these dams to release water, it flows down the Angat River system toward Bulacan's low-lying towns. At the same time, Meycauayan's waterways drain into Manila Bay — so during high tide, that outgoing flow slows or reverses, letting floodwater linger longer in the city.
